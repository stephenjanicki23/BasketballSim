      Tactics

      These are the inputs the engine reads on every possession. Schemes carry explicit effect tables — pace and space lifts three-point rate and pushes tempo while thinning the offensive glass; drop coverage trades three-point defense for rim protection. The sliders layer on top.

      Head Coach

      Every rating here is read somewhere in the simulation: offense and defense move what each side shoots, tactics drives turnovers and how fully a scheme's effects land, development sets how fast a player closes on his ceiling, leadership how quickly a locker room gels, and the scouting eye how accurate the club's read on potential is. Reputation is standing, not skill — it is drawn from the visible ratings with noise, so a coach can be over- or underrated.
